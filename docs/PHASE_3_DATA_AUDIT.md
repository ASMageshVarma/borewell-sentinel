## 3. Original Observations vs. Derived Features vs. Injected Scenarios

To maintain complete scientific honesty, every field in Research Mode is audited below:

### 3.1. Original Physical Observations (Empirical Grounding)
* **Depth to Water Level (`waterLevel` / `currentWaterLevelM`):** Measured in meters below ground level (`m bgl`). Sourced from piezoresistive hydrostatic pressure transducers suspended in CGWB observation piezometers.
* **Transducer Battery Voltage (`batteryVoltage` / `batteryVolts`):** Measured in Volts (`V`). Original downlink telemetry payload from solar-recharged lithium battery modules.
* **Station Coordinates (`lat`, `lon`):** Physical WGS84 coordinates of Tamil Nadu district monitoring stations (Perur, Omalur, Tiruchengode, Melur, etc.).
* **Well Depth (`depthM` / `wellDepthM`):** Drilled borehole casing depth in meters (`m`) from lithological completion reports.
* **Precipitation (`rainfallMm` / `rainfall14dMm`):** 24-hour and 14-day cumulative rainfall in millimeters (`mm`) interpolated from IMD $0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ gridded reanalysis.

### 3.2. Algorithmic Derived Features (Engineered Diagnostics)
These fields are **not raw sensor readings**; they are mathematical transformations computed deterministically by the Borewell Sentinel pipeline:
* **`delta7dM`:** Net 7-day water table elevation change ($\Delta d = d_t - d_{t-7}$). Negative indicates drawdown/depletion; positive indicates recharge.
* **`DATA QUALITY SCORE`:** Telemetry hygiene score ($0 - 100$) computed by [`lib/quality-engine.ts`](file:///c:/Users/MAGESH%20VARMA%20A%20S/Desktop/BOREWELL_SENTINEL_PROTOTYPE/lib/quality-engine.ts) through 8 deterministic rule penalties (missingness, duplicate, voltage, jumps, flatlines).
* **`seasonalBaseline` (Median & MAD):** Computed by [`lib/seasonal-baseline.ts`](file:///c:/Users/MAGESH%20VARMA%20A%20S/Desktop/BOREWELL_SENTINEL_PROTOTYPE/lib/seasonal-baseline.ts) using historical monthly series.
* **`robustZ`:** Robust Hampel z-score: $z = \frac{|x - \text{median}|}{1.4826 \times \text{MAD}}$.
* **`neighborAgreementPct`:** Percentage of cluster stations within 12 km exhibiting concordant directional change.
* **`persistenceDays`:** Rolling count of consecutive observations violating the seasonal expected range ($\pm 2.2 \times \text{MAD}$).

### 3.3. Calibrated Benchmark Injections (Algorithm Verification)
To rigorously evaluate whether the anomaly engine can distinguish sensor hardware failure from true aquifer depletion in the absence of continuous historical fault logs:
* **Station `TN-SL-04` (Salem):** Injected with a single-day uncalibrated transducer jump ($18.4$ m bgl) and corresponding battery brownout ($3.12$ V) to test sensor failure detection.
* **Station `TN-NK-05` (Namakkal):** Injected with multi-day persistent regional drawdown ($20.0 \to 22.8$ m bgl) and regional drought ($8$ mm rainfall) to test genuine aquifer depletion detection.
* **Station `TN-MD-12` (Madurai):** Injected with rainfall-drawdown contradiction ($95$ mm cloudburst with $14.8$ m drawdown) and isolated station spacing to test the `UNCERTAIN` classification.

> **Audit Confirmation:** These benchmark scenarios are explicitly cataloged in `data/sample/benchmark.json` and documented in the UI as *prototype evaluation cases*. They are not represented to users or judges as uncalibrated natural occurrences.

## 5. Measurement Units and Direction Audit

| Measurement | Unit | Physical Interpretation | Positive Direction ($+$) | Negative Direction ($-$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water Level Depth** | `m bgl` (meters below ground level) | Depth from ground surface to saturated water table | Deeper water table (greater depth below surface) | Shallower water table (closer to ground surface) |
| **7-Day Change ($\Delta 7d$)** | `meters (m)` | Water table elevation shift | Water table rise / aquifer recharge (favorable) | Water table drop / drawdown / depletion (stress) |
| **Precipitation** | `mm` (millimeters) | 24-hour or 14-day cumulative rainfall | Higher accumulated rainfall | Lower rainfall / drought |
| **Battery Voltage** | `V` (Volts) | Transducer telemetry unit lithium battery | $\ge 3.5$ V (Healthy) | $< 3.3$ V (Brownout / ADC drift risk) |
| **Quality Score** | Score ($0 - 100$) | Telemetry data hygiene | Higher data reliability | Higher telemetry defect probability |
